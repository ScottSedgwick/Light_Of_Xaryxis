# The sea is wet.

We're drowning.  Initiative: Dennis 8, Rusty 12, The Captain 19, Cleetus 21, Horus 30.

We all make Dex saves vs Fire - only Horus fails.  60 damage full, 30 half.

We all make Con saves vs Thunder -  we all save, but 1/2 damage is still 25.

## First round 

Horus grabs some sailors and starts to swim up with them.

Cletus swims down to the nearest sailors and grabs 4 sailors, and starts swimming up, then turns back down to seek more.

Horus grabs 4 and heads up.

The Captain stares longingly at the departing submarine, but chooses to save some of his crewmen instead.  He grabs 7 sailors and heads upward.

Rusty grabs 4 sailors and heads up.  Wit telekinetically grabs another 4 and moves them up.

Dennis swims downward sufficient distance to get to Louie which Ethan treats with disbelief.  But Dennis was right all along!

Raymond (who doesn't know where Louie is) swims up with 4 sailors.

Then a weird water elemental turns up and beats on The Captain.  It also tries a ranged attack on Rusty, but misses. Then is watery steps, so we don't know where it is (it's invisible in water).

The sailors all move toward the surface, to a greater or lesser extent.

## Second round

Horus casts Air Bubble around 2 of the free-swimming sailors, and the three he is carrying.

Cleetus swims across, drags some sailors up a bit, and lets them go.  They only have 10' to go to the surface.

The Captain extends his conga line to ridiculous length as he trawls the ocean for free-range sailors.

Rusty swims upward through the invisible water elemental, takes damage, all the sailors die.

Dennis swims upward with Louie and gets up to within 15' of the surface, and next to Raymond.  Raymond pulls them both to the surface.

The water elemental attacks Wit and Rusty, and knocks Wit down to death saves.  Then he teleports away.

The sailors swim up.

The elemental uses a legendary action to attack Cleetus.

## Third round

Horus casts Water Walk on a bunch of sailors and floats them directly to the surface.

Cleetus attacks the elemental. He misses once, hits once, misses once, action surges, hits 3 times with 1 crit.  First crit paralyzes him. Second crit reduces his AC by 1 (it's AC is now 21).

The Captain drags his conga line an insane distance upwards, about half way to the surface.

Rusty Misty Steps to Wit, casts Cure Wounds on Wit, and tows Wit a short way toward the surface.  Wit then casts Haste on himself, and tows them the rest of the way up.

Dennis Hand of Healing's Louie. Raymond hops toward the Water Elemental, Dennis flies the rest of the way and fucking smashes the Water Elemental to death.  He separates and keeps the flumph bits in case we want to resurrect them.

## End of combat.

We tie the flotsam together, and start towing it back to port.  When we get close enough, the non-racist sister sails her yacht out and picks us up.  We sail back to port.

The party takes a short rest.

# A three hour tour

The Captain takes some scuba gear. We pile on to the sisters yacht and follow the trail of flumph bodies.  Eventually it stops and goes straight down.  We swim down.  Several Con checks later, we reach the submarine.  We get into the airlock, and there is a sphincter you can stick your head into.  The Captain sticks his head in, and answers 3 questions, and is sucked into the sphincter.  That was gross, but Horus and Raymond follow suit.

We find ourselves in the air scrubbing room.  There is a janitor here - or was, before The Captain kills him with a single critical hit sneak attack to the back of the neck.  Rusty takes a moment to disable the scrubbers, and booby trap the emergency oxygen supply.

The next room is the waste disposal room.  Rusty sees the remains of some flumphs soon to be ejected from the ship, and unbolts the drain cover to retrieve their bodies.  Horus spots a prototype psionic weapon on the bench, and retrieves it.  There are notes about it, written in future Celestial.  Horus manages to decipher it. It says the device is unstable, and it has been abandoned after 3 subject were unintentially liquified.  It is activated by holding it, and pointing it.  We _think_ we know which end is the dangerous end.  Horus picks it up, and it hums ominously.  We keep it.

# Air =/= Oxygen

It is clear that Ethan does not understand gas dynamics.  This is good and bad, in equal parts.  We get through sector F eventually.  We find a room filled with baby flumphs - 33 of them - in glass prisons.  After a while, we let them out.  They go insane. Fibblewink et al.

We trap them all in the room, and ferry them 3 at a time (the most Wix can restrain with Telekinesis) to the escape capsules and seal them in.  Each of the capsules can take 8 baby flumphs.  We load them up, and launch them when they are full.  There's only space in the capsules for 32 flumphs, though, so one will have to remain behind.  Rusty chooses to keep the responsible one.

While Rusty and Wix do this, Horus and Cleetus find a door with glowing handprints. As you put your hands into the prints, different shapes appear.  Cleetus relays direction to Horus - Horus complies and draws the pictures. The doors open to a ladder to the next deck.
