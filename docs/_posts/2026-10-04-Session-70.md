# Grafters Anonymous

There is a control panel in the lab that seems to allow you to graft Flumph parts on to recipients.  The instructions are spartan - "Put the recipient on top of the canister, then you choose what type of grafting part that you want."

| Number | Effect |
| ------ | ------ |
| 1 | Flumph luminous slime. Any target hit with a natural attack will not be able to benefit from Invisibility. |
| 2 | Once per round, you can exploit another creature's emotional state to move without provoking opportunity attacks. |
| 3 | Allows the graftee to squeeze through narrow (1" wide) openings as a gas. |
| 4 | If a creature fails a WIS save, they laugh and lose their reaction until next turn. |
| 5 | Gain advantage on Persuasion checks. |
| 6 | Once per turn, use bonus action to give a nearby ally +1 AC. |
| 7 | If you roll a natural 1, instead of automatic fail, Wild Magic goes off. |

There are others, if we have suggestions.  Charybdis asks if there is anything that can allow Thyssara to control her petrification stank.  It turns out there is a "remove graft" option.  Charybdis asks if she wants that - she seems nervous of the machines, but wants to try.  Charybdis helps her onto the platform and removes the graft.

The removal process has to remove most of her legs.  Cybernetic legs are being built in the corner - why not a snake tail?  Nobody knows.  It is painful - Charybdis tells her it is almost over. The entire process takes over a quarter of an hour - 17 minutes - or 11.3 moments.

Outside, the Kuotoa are trying to break through a door - to the Taxidermy room.  They are trying to take the failed imaginations that they made for Xalititty. The Captain interrupts them, and attempts to persuade them not to, but they're not having it.  The Captain, however, is extremely persuasive.

Rusty skims the list looking for advantages to psychic magic.  There is one thing - an aura that has a 10 foot radius, which allows anyone in the aura to automatically succeed on their save against Dominate type spells.

Rusty selects the "On a Nat 1, it's not a failure necessarily, but you do get a Wild Magic surge."  The process takes 4-5 minutes.  The liquid that gets implanted has stars and mushrooms in it.  The process is successful and substantially less painful that removal.

Wit is next for the aura. About 4-5 minutes go by, Wit is a little nervous.  It is successful.

The party decides to leave the trashing of this room to the end, after they clear the rest.

The chests brought in by Fob and Gob apparently contained the bodies. The DM explicitly states there is nothing of interest in here. # To the Chime Room

Initiative! That worked well. Rusty: 31. The Captain: 33. Louie: 28. Horus: 27. Charybdis: 20.

## Round One

Inside is a mutated Will-o-wisp.  

The Captain sneak attacks it to great effect.  

Then Rusty goes in, offers to un-mutate it, which it refuses.  Wit casts Haste on Rusty, Rusty enters the room, takes 16 cold damage, misses three times and is totally ripped off with regards to his Wild Magic surge.  

Louie and Raymond enter the room and both fail their saves against cold and have their movement rates reduced to 0. Then Louie misses all his attacks.

Horus edges around the door and fires Witch Bolt - he misses, but Rusty Flash of Genius's him, and turns it into a hit.  6 lightning damage. Unfortunately, Will O Wisps are immune to lightning damage.  He does notice that it has an airy movement - it's probably vulnerable to Thunder damage.

Charybdis enters the room and moves up next to the Will O Wisp.  He turns his arm into a whip and whips the crap out of it.  Charybdis takes 9 cold damage, and does 8 slashing damage.  So, a net loss, really.  He swings again, but misses.  Then again, with a Nat 20! 12 slashing damage, and a card: Conjuror. Additional 3d12 damage: 24. Nice! He hits once more for 6 more damage, taking 10 in return.  Then he Action Surges! One miss, 2 hits!  He does 15, but takes 5.

Then it's the Wisp's turn. First attack vs Charybdis: 25. Hit! He only has a 17 AC. He takes 34 cold damage, which is just enough to drop him. As he falls, he is turned to gaseous form.  Then it attacks Rusty. 32 - hits, doing 31 points of cold damage, and turns gaseous. Lastly, Raymond: 28 hits, 28 cold damage halved.  In gaseous form, we have a flying speed of 10 feet, cannot attack or cast spells, have resistance to non-magical damage, and advantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution saves. Rusty gets sucked into the Will O Wisp. He is blinded and restrained, and will take damage at the start of each of my turns.

## Round Two

The Captain attempts to Clock of Billowing the gaseous personnel out of the room.  He does 10 piercing + 4 (8) thunder damage plus some trash sneak damage + 10 for the bow.  When he hits, the Wisp enlarges, contracts, enlarges, contracts, then spits Rusty out in his normal form and he takes 7 fall damage.

Rusty hits him twice, takes a little damage. With Mind Slivers him, he takes damage and -2 to his next save.

Louie and Raymond take cold damage to start. Louie lays on hands on the gaseous, unconscious Charybdis, healing him for 50 HP and dispelling the gaseous effect on him. Raymond rolls a CON save and remains gaseous.

Horus steps up to the doorway and casts Chromatic Orb.  He rolls a 29 to hit, and does 3d8 thunder damage - 18, doubles to 36.

The Wisp explodes, doing 37 points of healing to everyone in the room, and removing one level of exhaustion from Louie.

The Captain bottles the essence of wisp, getting a bottle of 90 hit points.

The Rakshasa was excited, but the party's destruction of the sub, the domination of Charybdis' mind was broken.  Now, killing or subverting all his monsters has significantly reduced his power against Barovia, and now we have destroyed his genetics labs, he cannot create more minions.

Eating the Rakshasa also makes Charybdis significantly more powerful.

We enter the University. We visit the research thesis room and each get a cool thing.

Then we go to the office that The Captain's magic rod points at, and we figure it is a false office.  The Captain finds the secret door behind the bookcase.  We all go down the stairs into the Batcave.

It is stupidly dark.  Charybdis starts to glow. Louie, who was standing next to him, gets temporarily blinded.  There are animals clinging to the walls in the darkness, mutated and with Illithid tadpoles inside them.  There is also an Illithid in a tank that is too far along to reverse.  It communicates with sign language: "Please". They point toward a valve on the side of the tank - it appears to be the valve that controls oxygen flow to the tank.  They painfully suffocates to death, but the last thing they do before dying is sign "Thank You".

As we proceed, we can see what all the dominated artificers were working on.  Further ahead is a portcullis blocking the tunnel.  Water flows through it, and next to it are 3 valves labeled a, b and c.  Scratched in to the wall are the numbers 1, 2 and 3.  A:3, B:1, C:2 (from left to right). Louie figures it out. The portcullis raises.

# Ths Final Room

Inside the room is a large framework, filled with Psychic energy. The energy is emanating from 2 Flumphs that are connected to them. Mummy! Daddy! They looked tired, malnourished and drained. We are inundated with overlapping thoughts from what are obviously different domains.

Horus searches for stuff. He finds a note addressed to "L.V.Z". It says "The destroyer at the end of time did not relinquish Ooblex. Whilst they are in the domain of dread we should consider Ooblex contained. This may change if they leave a domain of dread. The destroyer declined to deliver the Ooblex to us."

The Captain searches the room for Xalititty, but cannot see him.

Charybdis activates True Sight. This reveals Xalititty to his sight, where The Captain is standing.

Louie was searching for anything in the room that could be helpful.  He steps into a small fountain in the corner of the room, and is instantly healed of all the remaining exhaustion.  He bottles the fountain water, and gets 4 potions of Greater Restoration.

Rusty investigates the Framework to figure out how to extract his parents safely. Rusty's parents think he is a hallucination - their tendrils reach out to the glass of their cages.  In the stress of the moment, Rusty cannot work out how to extract them without killing them. 

Next to The Captain, Xalititty appears. His hands rests on the control panel for the Framework. He activates a lever, which releases Rusty's parents.

"You have all gone to a tremendous amount of work to stop the work that would have benefitted every creature in the shadow realms. You are on the wrong side. You have destroyed an army purely designed to combat Strahd. You have disabled the only communication network capable of bypassing the limitations of the shadow realms."

"Xellix cloned himself mani times before becoming a lich.  I trust that my brilliance is working wherever I am. So take me to Bane.  But know that your actions have made enemies. My allies will come for you. I don't care what you do with me, but I will go out with a bang."

The moment he released Rusty's parents, the psychic migraine stopped. Xalititty surrenders.  He holds out his hands.  There appears to be no trick.  The Captain puts the manacles on Xalititty - the special psychic control manacles that Bane gave us.

As Bane takes him away, Charybdis Stops him and demands to know Why?  Why were going to kill Thyssara?  He responds that Charybdis was his greatest creation, and he was going to be the saviour of us all.  Rusty tells Xalititty that his clones would never be a problem for us, nor us for them.  We didn't chase him for principled reasons, we did it for personal ones. And he makes The Captain promise to make whatever Bane does _painful_.  He tells Charybdis not to ever feel guilt or concern over Xalititty's claims of being his progenitor - Xalititty didn't do *shit*. Three Kuo Toa were his creators.

# To the Docks

The Captain takes Xalititty to the docks where he meets with Oriana.  They get a rowboat, and shove Xalititty in.  The Captain, Oriana, Sun and Xalititty row into the mists.

After a few hours of rowing, they find themselves in a ruined temple of Selune. Sun has been regaling them with details of Bane's worship.  The Captain decides to cut this voyage short, and they corrupt the sacred symbol of the temple with a Black Hand. They work to convert it to Bane's worship. 

The Captain says "By Eye and by Blood, by Conquest and Command, I cast down the mercy of the moon maiden. No longer shall the lost find refuge here. No longer shall the weak seek protection beneath her light. May the silver moon be bound by the iron fist. Let hope give way to obedience. May every stone, every shadow, know it's true master. By the will of the Black Hand I claim this sanctuary. In Bane's name, let all kneel."

The sanctuary transforms, and a shadow Bane appears. Sun pledges himself to Bane's worship.  Bane grins at The Captain and Xalititty.  Bane says "Well done, Captain. Let me fix your illness." The Captain is purged of xeremorphosis.
