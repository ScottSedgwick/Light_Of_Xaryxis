# Fuck that sub

The Captain stealths and vanishes.  Why? Nobody knows.  We load the escape pod with healthy thralls, and Rook and Cova.  Then launch them into the ocean.

The majority of the party then head down to the airlock, while The Captain waits behind to shoot the psychic reservoir.  He does so, then races to the airlock.  There is a tense moment when he fails his ability check to pass through the mirror, but a judicious use of Lucky gets him through.

The party escapes the sub, then traverses horizontally to open the range from the sub without risking the bends.  After a few minutes it explodes.  We then go up to the surface, waiting the appropriate amount of time at the appropriate levels to allow Nitrogen to naturally precipitate out of the bloodstream via the lungs.

# Back to town

When we reach the surface, all the Flumphlings have already disembarked from their escape pods and boarded the yacht.  We get Rook, Cova and the thralls aboard too and head back to town.

Dennis is keeping watch over Louie, and he begins to recover on the way back to town.  Louie looks significantly worse for wear.  He has 5 levels of exhaustion.

The Captain decides we are heading to the Isle of Agony.  Raymond is carrying Louie who has no movement speed.  On the way out of Ludendorf, they run into Courier, who asks them if we know a guy named "Malar".  Malar sent a message via Courier, that member of Baytown have been disappearing, then coming back a few days later but changed.

We wait for the tide, then walk to Baytown.  There are a couple of Flesh Golems there. We meet Elara, the inkeeper.  She asks how we are going.  The Captain rolls Investigation on her, and determines she is a Lycanthrope.  It's on now.  Then he looks again, and she isn't now.  Weird.  The Captain asks about the missing people.  Gob and Fob are missing, mostly because the town shuns them for having awful, unimaginative names.  When they show up, they're different.  They've also got really into swimming, and regularly help unload a submarine the passes by.  That's not coming back :)  

Sounds like an Aboleth to me.

Elara also mentions to Rusty that many of her patrons are getting less sick these days, and she thanks him for putting doorways that are up to code between her sanitaion and food prep areas. +30 Lamode.

She takes a look at Louie and gets inappropriately intimate.  Then she approaches Raymond and stands on tiptoe to whisper to Louie that he looks like shit.  She advises he pays a visit to the moonstones, where a gift or prayer to Selune might do something useful.  Louie heads over there and begs like a beggar for any help. None is forthcoming.

There is a hatch in the ground, which opens to a sloping tunnel.  Rusty notices the three triangles symbol in the grass - it looks like the other end of one of the teleporation cirles in the house in Ludendorf. The party enters the tunnel.

We reach a cramped alcove.  The door that Charybdis fled through is now closed.  Slumped against one wall is a Duergar.  Duergar are not native to Lamordia, so he must be a traveller.  There is a journal written in Dwarvish on his chest.  The Captain inspects the body - it is dead, but there are no wounds.  It's like his heart just stopped.
