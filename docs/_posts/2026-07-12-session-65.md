# Continuing the puzzle quest

Rusty and Louie (and Raymond, Minion, Wit - hell, most of the party) find themselves before 2 pedestals.  Before them is a sign that reads "D&Deopardy!"  It's a game of Jeopardy, except:

* You don't have to answer inthe form of a question
* The questions are, in large part, about us
* Alex Trebek is nowhere to be seen

There are 4 columns of questions: Lore, Moments, Last Hits and People; and each column has scores from 100 to 500.  Despite having it, Rusty does *not* spam "Keen Mind" to get the DM to tell him the answers.

I can't remember all the questions & answers (there were 20 of them) and in any case, the 3 score keepers all had different totals when we finished.  However, Louie won.

The first round!  Final D&Deopardy has you betting some or all of your score, on one final question.  All or nothing!

Louie won that one too.

Rusty gets a small coin purse with 50 GP in it as a consolation prize.  Louie wins 4500 GP and a permanent +2 to his History skill.

A door behind the game board opens, and show us:

# The Bridge of Suspended Disbelief

Beyond the door is a transparent bridge, stretching to infinity.  We start walking - almost immediately, everyone has to make saves. Wit is the only failure - 66 Force damage.  

The next round of saves were awesome. 2 Natural 20's.  This sparked an insight - we rolled it, and realized the bridge was, in fact, an illusion.  We fall a short distance onto a platform.

On the platform is a person, waiting for us - but that person appears differently to each viewer.  Louie sees Bhaal, Rusty sees Xal'ititty, Wit sees a Beholder, and Raymond sees an enormous blowfly.  They each say things to us that are designed to get a rise out of us.  We immediately respond with complementary insults of our own, which triggers the actual battle - it's like a Rap Battle, but with illusions that cause actual consequences.  We're all flinging descriptive insults at each other and rolling contested insight checks.  We're better an insight than they are, are tear them apart with our godlike articulation.  Rusty briefly considers taking Bard levels.

# The party grows some more

A door opens at the edge of the platform room.  Through the doorway we see a large room with an 8x7 grid of 5x5 foot tiles.  At the doorway is a pedestal with a glowing skull resting on it.

The party has learned its lesson about: 1) touching skulls on pedestals; and 2) stepping on obviously trapped tiles.  So Wit picks the skull up, from range, using Telekinesis.  The skull cries out "Unhand me, you foolish creature!"  We recognise Sinned's voice.  We never liked that guy, and now we have a new trapfinder!  Wit proceeds to telekinetically bounce Sinned off tiles, finding a safe path across. Too easy.

Turns out there was a trick - the safe tiles spelled "LiE" - but ain't nobody got time for that.

I think Ethan said that the skull disappeared after that, which was disappointing.  Rusty was looking forward to abusing Sinned's mortal remains for some time to come.

# This time, there are instructions

The next room is a faded chapel to Leira.  There is a statue of her, with a plaque: "Lady who is never what she seems, I greet you with my face hidden from the sun. Let today's truths bend where they need to bend. Let my words serve me better than they serve the world. Cloak what should stay hidden, and give value to the things I keep concealed. I lie before you, as I lie for you."

It took some doing, but we figured it out.  It was a bit too literal for Rusty, but we followed the instructions and a manhole opens up.

# Fucking Weeping Angels!

3 of them.  In the next room.  Oddly, they are talking a bit. "I don't like being here." "Fuck the Gods." "I'm tired of being toyed with."  There's a plaque that reads "Soothe their sorrow to find your path."

Reading Ethan's notes, we were supposed to tell them soothing lies.

Rusty takes things more literally, and has Wit _Banish_ the one that doesn't want to be here.  That solves her problem, but the others didn't like that at all and start trying to petrify us.  So Wit drops concentration and brings her back.  This makes things even worse.  _Fine!_  Wit upcasts _Banish_ and gets rid of all 3 of them, then holds concentration to make sure they stay there.

Confounded, Ethan has the stairway to the next room open up.

# Boss fight

The next room contains Liera, who tells us that the 2 shards we have gathered are sufficient, and now she's going to kill us for them.  Initiative!

Rusty does really well and gets a dirty 20.  Louie says "Suck on this" and gets 26.  Liera says "Suck harder" and gets 31.  _Fine_.

## Round 1

Liera laughs, giggles, and casts some sort of fog cloud that obscures her position.  She then takes a swing at Louie and would have hit, had Rusty not used his Reaction to cast _Shield_.  She then hits us all with _Synaptic Static_.  Rusty and Louie fail their saves and are muddled.

Louie casts _Zone of Truth_ over where Liera is.  It seemed like a good idea at the time.

Wit cast _Reverse Gravity_, but Liera makes her save and clings to her throne.  Rusty casts _Fireball_ and it does double damage!  Wit takes note and uses Metamagic to cast _Firestorm_ with his bonus action.  It's super effective!

## Round 2

Liera hits Louie and Rusty with a Cloak of Mist, then dispels Louie's Zone of Truth.  This allows her to tell us that we have no mouths any more - Rusty attempts to disbelieve and takes an automatic 67 damage, because fuck him, that's why.

Raymond has no mouth, but a prior conversation with Ethan explicitly says that he was holding his sword by the hilt, and the blade was pointing out of his mouth.  So he now has a Unicorn Horn made of magical steel.  **Charge!**

That egregious skewering is enough to finish Liera off.  Surprise - it wasn't real!  Yeah, we knew.  The false Liera shatters into a bunch of mirror shards, which Minion collects.  We have 5, and each one can be used for True Seeing and are worth 500gp each.  It is not explicitly specified that they are consumable, so maybe we just have 5 sets of goggles of true seeing.  :)

From the center of the illusionary Liera falls a shard of perfect clarity.  Rusty catches it in a Gyroscope.  This one grants immunity to illusion magic while held.

Jergal's voice rings out: "You may do as you see fit."

Then Liera's voice rings out "Return to me, bearers of truth and deception. Let us conclude our arrangement."

# Honouring our deal, because that's what heroes do.

We return to Liera, and quickly do a bit of contract clarification.  She agrees that Wit and Raymond count as party members, so the party gets 4 favours overall.  To recap:

<details><summary>Leira's rewards</summary>
Each character will receive one favour:
<ul>
  <li>The answer to any question, guaranteed truthful</li>
  <li>An illusion that can deceive even divine sight, lasting 1d4 days after activating.</li>
  <li>The ability to detect any lie told in their presence for 1d6 days after activating.</li>
  <li>Safe passage through any deception-based challenge they will face in the future</li>
  <li>Knowledge of a secret that could change the course of their quest</li>
</ul>
</details>

These favours can be redeemed at any time, in any place.

In addition, she grants us one illusion boon for the party, when we want one.  We should get clarity from Ethan as what "illusion" means, but it should be pretty good.

She also tells us:

> Your presence here was no accident. Someone wants you to find something - something related to Bhaal.  3 days in that direction is a colour portal that leads to Bhaal's domain. There you will find answers to questions you haven't even thought to ask.

# Getting the gang back together

We exit the demiplane. Wit gets in touch with Horus & The Captain, and Horus teleports them in.  We head in the direction of the profesized portal, and after one day of travel, Louie and Rusty simultaneously fall catatonic.  They come to on a floor of black glass beneath a sky of roiling fire. Hephaestus and Selune stand before them. Rusty kneels before Hephaestus (it seems appropriate) and Hephaestus tells him to rise.

"We've been monitoring what you have been doing.  We will show you what you have accomplished."

We see a vision of Bhaal, walking into a perfectly innocent city that hadn't done anything wrong.  Bhaal proceeds to kill every single person in the city.  Not even in a fun way, they just fall down when they get too close.  Rusty is pretty sure he sees Bhaal heading off for a wank - but then the loss of the domains of lies, treachery and illusion hits him and the mood is just _ruined_.

The vision fades, and Selune & Hephaestus say "We have a plan. We want you to sever the connection to his avatar."
