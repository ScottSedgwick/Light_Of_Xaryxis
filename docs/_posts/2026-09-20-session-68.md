# Free the Flumphs!

Noodlestare - the only sensible flumphling of the lot - remains with the party. All the rest we are ejecting into the ocean.  They are running amok.

Horus has unlocked the puzzle door and finds a ladder going up to the next deck of the sub. He looks back toward the chaos and notes that all of the baby flumphs are uniquely mutated. Fibblewink, for instance, can teleport, leaving behind a translucent after-image. Jellywisps body is partially liquified. Twinkledrift can split into duplicates that are them re-absorbed. Horvil generates a tiny localised gravitational field. Whistlepod makes a whistling sounds several seconds before danger, and Hushbloom gives off a violet glow that absorbs sound.

Raymond's tongue reaches for Whistlepod, and Whistlepod whistles.  Staggering toward Horus is The Janitor that we killed earlier.  He telepathically says "Destroyer at the end of time. It's ironic, when you think about it. You are compromised.  Tell you what. Let me take him off your hands, and I'll make sure he doesn't escape his prison."  Horus pretends he can't hear Xalititty.

As Rusty goes for the next batch of flumphs, he spots the discussion between the Janitor and Horus. He can tell the Janitor is not undead - he's being telekinetically moved. "Rusty, these are not your siblings.  Aren't you a monster hunter?  We have the same goal!" A long conversation ensues where Xalititty unintentionally reveals his growing desperation.  He's practically begging for us to back off. "I've given you so many chances, Flumph.  I believe the only options is to reset your identity."

# Trust fall

There is a flash of psychic light.  When Horus regains his vision, Rusty is gone. Beside where he was is a glowing snowglobe. 

Horus looks into the Snowglobe. It looks like the Feywild  He sees three of Rusty's things, and in the centre, Rusty - but naked.  He is wandering aimlessly. Horus tries to talk to him, but there is no recognition. 

Rusty cannot see or hear anything.  He is blind and deaf. He is also sort of feebleminded - his brain is foggy, and he's not sure what is happening.  He feels a light feeling of being at home, and he is cold. He doesn't know who he is. He reaches out with his mind, but senses nothing.  Then he hears static, and Wit connects Rusty's mind with Horus' mind.  Rusty can see Horus in his mind.  Horus is an 8' sewed together living corpse. Obviously trustworthy.

Horus directs Rusty through some sort of maze.  He's getting Rusty close to the exit when Rusty suddenly takes 40 psychic damage, and outside the psychic world two of Rusty's siblings (Blueray and Blupernug) are killed by psychic attacks.  A bit later Rusty takes more psychic damage and Fizzesoft dies.

We continue - there is some interference - but eventually Horus guides Rusty through the maze, the snowglobe cracks, and Rusty is released, recovering all his memories. Wit, assisted by Rusty, defibrillates all of his murdered brethren.

# Finding Cova

Fizzlesoft asks if Cova is in her room.  Cova is apparently another Flesh Golem.  We don't know, but we get the remaining kids onto the escape pods and launch them before going into Cova's room.

Cova's room is a fucking nightmare.  It looks like a serial killer's wet dream.  There is a half constructed golem on the table, with purple maggots crawling through the body.

Cova warns us to prepare, so we get advantage on our save against her sonic attack.  We both save, but take half damage each.  Initiative!  Horus 31. Rusty 23. Cova, less.

## Round 1

Horus ducks inside and pulls the lever. It's icky, and nothing happens. He takes 9 psychic damage. He then shoots Cova with a Firebolt.

Rusty hits and takes a bit of retributive damage, then crits, and in addition to doing 67 HP damage, draws the Seer card, making him immune to 1d4 damage types for 1d4 rounds (Piercing, Poison and Acid for 2 rounds). Then he misty steps up into the corner of the ceiling.

Cova hits Horus, but doesn't do much damage.  Then she tries to grappling hook Rusty, but misses. Then she hits Horus again, and part of the damage is Fire, so Horus is at disadvantage until the end of his next turn. He consumes one instance of fire resistance, and removes the disadvantage.  The she cries like a sook.

## Round 2

Horus goes for a disarm on the cleaver hand, because that's the one being Heat Metalled.  He succeeds, and she loses the Cleaver.

Wit maintains concentration on Heat Metal, and hits her with Mind Sliver. That's when we find she is vulnerable to psychic.  Rusty hits her with the Cerebral Circlet, which knocks her out.

# Rook, recovered

We tie her up, and look for a bag to put over her head.  We find a bag of Doritos, which are solidified healing potions.  We eat them (10d6 healing each) and then fix Rook up.

Rook says that Rusty's parents were forced to reproduce, then sent to a science city somewhere.  Rusty's siblings are used for experiments.  They dump the failed experiments into the sea.

Rook is also immune to psychic, which prevents Xalititty from controlling him.  He wishes to rest, so we untie him from the gurney, give him Cova's weapons, and Cova, and leave him to recover.

# Level 2

We go up the ladder to the second level, and find ourselves in a mirrored hallway with no apparent exits.  Each mirror has a set of thought-provoking questions written on it, and when we think of our answers to those questions, an image of what we think of appears.

An old guy in a wheelchair passes through the mirror. After extensive experimentation, we determine that the ability to pass through the mirror is something to do with him, not his wheelchair, and while his wheelchair can pass through if he does, nothing else can.  Rusty attempts to dis-believe illusion, but that doesn't work - but it makes Rusty think that this is something thought related.  He thinks of an open doorway, which does show an image of it in the mirror, but it does not allow Rusty to pass through.  Meditating to think of nothing does work, allowing Wit to push Rusty through. Wit then uses Rusty's senses to look around, picks up Horus, and Dimension Door's to Rusty's location.

# Restrain the thralls

We find a bunch of other controlled people working the sub.  We tie them up and restrain them in the crew quarters.  We find a scholar that has been driven insane by Xalititty trying to teach him future Deep Speech, and one thing he mutters is "the levers".  Once all the thralls are tied up, we start to search for a way to the next level.  Rusty makes a dirty 20 on his Investigation roll, but still fails, because it is a natural 1.

None the less, Horus spots the way up.  It is a fleshy panel in the ceiling with no way to open it.  From the crew quarters we hear "the levers!".  Yeah, that aint happening.  Rusty gathers up some cable from around the place, stacks up some boxes under the fleshy panel, and uses a chunk of wood to wedge one end of the cable into the flesh.  Then he runs the cable out to the other cabin section that clearly shows exposed lightning bolts powering - something.  Whatever, Rusty throws the other end of the cable in and watches the sparks fly.  They panel contracts open and stays there.

# Useless control panel

Up top we find a Illithid control panel and a tungsten shielded cylinder of psychic power.  The cylinder is releasing a pulse of psychic power every turn.  With casts Intellect Fortress on the party to give us resistance.  Long story short - we have to figure out how to destroy the cylinder, which will destroy the sub in about 5 minutes.

Wit casts Widogast's Vault of Amber on the top bit of the Tungsten shield, stealing a bit over 20cm of the top of the tungsten shield.  This exposes enough of the inner container to make it easy to break.  Also, Rusty has ~260kg of worked Tungsten, which is priceless.

Also, we were _supposed_ to be trapped there.  If we had opened the panel by pulling the levers, the panel would have closed behind us.  Because we are constantly electrocuting it, though, we can easily back off to consider our options.

We have 22 thralls, Rook, Cova, Horus, Rusty, Wit, Raymond and The Captain aboard.  There is one escape pod at the back of the bridge that can hold 11.  Hmmmm.

We decide that Rook, Cova and the healthiest of the thralls should go in the pod.  The party can escape with the Water Breathing they had on them - it's still running.  Now we are filtering through the thralls - and we notice one is missing.  

# Sabotage

The scribe guy is not here.  It's pretty obvious where he has gone - Xalititty would have sent him down to the airlock to trap us inside.  Horus and Rusty race down.  We pass Rook and Cova on the way - Rusty shouts instructions to them to go up to the mirror chamber - their way out is on the other side, Rusty will be back soon to show them the way through.

Horus and Rusty reach the airlock chamber with no time to spare.  Horus casts Hold Person, and Rusty casts Levitate.  The scribe _saves_ against Horus' spell with a natural twenty, but fails his save against Rusty with a 12.  He is flung into the deckhead, breaks his neck and dies.
